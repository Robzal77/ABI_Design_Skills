# abi-brand-system

## When to use
Apply ABI identity to any artifact.

## Method
Use brand/brand.json and brand/PROVENANCE.md. Public verified rules govern colors. Do not add a logo, corporate typeface or specific gradient without verified assets. Mark template approval pending.

## Validation and handoff
Check accessible contrast and brand provenance.

## Governance
Follow AGENTS.md and brand/PROVENANCE.md. Do not invent claims, assets, approvals or functionality. Save editable source and identify remaining manual checks.
