# abi-content-story

## When to use
Structure narratives and editorial content.

## Method
Convert user source materials into headline, audience, purpose, points and evidence; don't make up quantified value. Verify wording and citations before design.

## Validation and handoff
Use examples/brief.json as input shape.

## Governance
Follow AGENTS.md and brand/PROVENANCE.md. Do not invent claims, assets, approvals or functionality. Save editable source and identify remaining manual checks.
