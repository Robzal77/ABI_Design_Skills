# Brand provenance and review policy

## Verified official AB InBev public sources
- [Creative Hub](https://brandworld.ab-inbev.com/)
- [Color](https://brandworld.ab-inbev.com/color) — primary black and white; prominent gold gradient; secondary accents; tertiary colors mainly for charts.
- [Typography](https://brandworld.ab-inbev.com/typography) — corporate typeface **Avantt**; layout/type hierarchy and one headline graphic device at most. No font binaries are bundled.
- [Logo](https://brandworld.ab-inbev.com/logo) — symbol + wordmark; gold, black and white alternatives; preserve measured clear space; top-left or lower sign-off placement; do not stretch or manipulate. Expressive symbol variants require special marketing/brand permission.
- [Photography](https://brandworld.ab-inbev.com/photos) — style reference, not unrestricted image license.
- [Applications](https://brandworld.ab-inbev.com/applications) — brand applications and creative references.
- [Resources](https://brandworld.ab-inbev.com/resources) — downloadable resources require separate access/license verification.

## Corporate colors from official color page
Primary: black #000000, white #FFFFFF, gold family #E5B611, #F5E003, #D1A33C.
Light grey #EFEFEF; dark grey #C7C7C7; red #921A28. Additional chart colors are in brand.json.
Core logo assets and prescribed gold-gradient geometry should be sourced from an approved kit; no approximated logo or asset files in repo.

## Corporate examples and internal template
The user supplied sample screenshots, and has confirmed possession of an approved company PPT template. These are useful **references**, but template masters, embedded assets, font file rights, composition rules and editable components have not yet been extracted. Sample screenshots are not an official asset license.

## Gate and usage
The Creative Hub presents an age-of-legal-consumption gate. Publicly indexed sections are readable for research, but this does not grant permission to redistribute protected assets. Use only when legally permitted under jurisdictional rules.

## Release guard
`productionApproved=false` until internal template and assets are supplied and reviewed. Public color references alone do not meet internal production sign-off. Do not store font files in this repository without licensing.
